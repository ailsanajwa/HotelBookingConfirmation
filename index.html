ABOUT THE RESIDENCE

A quiet 19th-century sanctuary perched above the limestone cliffs of Cassis.

Built in 1887 from local blond stone and terracotta, Maison Soleil has been carefully restored into an intimate six-room boutique retreat. Shaded by ancient olive trees and fragrant with wild lavender and rosemary, the house offers sweeping views over the Mediterranean and the dramatic red limestone of Cap Canaille.

CONCIERGE GUIDE · CASSIS & PROVENCE

Around Town.

HANDPICKED BY MARGAUX 5–15 MINS FROM VILLA

Our favorite spots in Cassis: from morning café au lait to secret calanques swims.

No tourist traps—just the intimate bistros, hidden rocky coves, and scenic vineyards that we frequent ourselves.

CAFÉS & BAKERIES 4 MIN WALK

Boulangerie Saint-Michel

The oldest village bakery in Cassis. Pick up fresh fougasse with black olives and warm pain au chocolat before morning swims.

✦ Must try: Pain au levain & Tropezienne tart
QUAYSIDE COFFEE 6 MIN WALK

Café de la Marine

Pull up a wicker chair under the awnings facing the old port. Watch fishermen unload the morning catch over a café serré.

✦ Best time: 08:30 AM for morning sun
RESTAURANTS 8 MIN WALK

Chez Gilbert

Charter member of the authentic Marseille Bouillabaisse association. Exceptional saffron broth, fresh rockfish, and garlic rouille.

✦ Reservations recommended 24h prior
NATURE & COVES 45 MIN HIKE / BOAT

Calanque d’En-Vau

A dramatic limestone fjord with turquoise waters. We provide woven beach mats and bottled chilled water for your hike.

✦ Wear sturdy shoes for the descent
LOCAL BEACH 7 MIN WALK

Plage du Bestouan

Polished pebble beach right by the villa with unforgettable views looking straight across at the glowing red cliffs of Cap Canaille.

✦ Sunset aperitif on the rocks
TRANSPORTATION AT THE PORT

Harbor Shuttles & Gare SNCF

Scenic boat shuttles depart every 45 mins from the quayside. Cassis train station is 10 mins by electric bus M1 to Marseille.

✦ Ask reception for bus timetable
